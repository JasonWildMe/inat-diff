How this map is built

  • iNaturalist: research-grade US observations. Records with obscured locations, or whose location uncertainty could place them in more than one county, are skipped.
  • EDDMapS: reviewed reports with a positive status and a date known to the day. The county is the one EDDMapS reports. Copies of iNaturalist records (identified from their metadata) are skipped so they are not counted twice.
  • GBIF: preserved museum specimens only, with a full date, excluding copies of iNaturalist records.
  • A nightly job rebuilds each source when it is due for refresh. A county's first record is the earliest date across the sources you select.

Caveats

  • Observer effort. Counties with few observers look later than they are.
  • Records are not established populations. A single specimen, a dead insect found in a shipment, or a misidentification can set a county's date. Click a county to check its records.
  • Boundaries. Counties use 2020 Census boundaries.
