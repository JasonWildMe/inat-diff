Subscribe to Invasive Species Alerts

Get automated reports of previously unreported species on iNaturalist delivered to your inbox. These reports are designed to help you utilize iNaturalist to monitor for potential invasive species.

Enter a state, country, or specific region recognized by iNaturalist. Examples: "Oregon", "United States", "Multnomah County", "Yellowstone National Park"

Leave blank to monitor all species, or enter a taxonomic group to focus your reports.

How It Works

  1. Subscribe - Enter your email and region above
  2. Verify - Click the link in your confirmation email
  3. Receive Reports - Get automated reports showing species observed in your region that weren't seen in the previous 20 years

What You'll Get

  • List of new species observations in your region
  • Direct links to each observation on iNaturalist
  • Common and scientific names
  • Observation counts

Note: Reports can take 30-120 minutes to generate depending on the region size, as we query 20 years of historical iNaturalist data.
